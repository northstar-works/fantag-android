Delete these files if they exist in your older Android repo before building b78 stable-no-push:

app/src/main/kotlin/com/northstarlabs/fantag/FantagMessagingService.kt
app/src/main/kotlin/com/northstarlabs/fantag/PushRegistrar.kt
app/src/main/kotlin/com/northstarlabs/fantag/FantagNotifications.kt
app/google-services.json

Also make sure these are NOT present:
- Firebase dependencies in app/build.gradle
- Google Services plugin in app/build.gradle or root build.gradle
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission in AndroidManifest.xml
- Firebase messaging <service> block in AndroidManifest.xml
- notification/push preferences in app/src/main/res/xml/preferences.xml
